# Care of copper

## 1. Tarnish

Copper darkens with air and water. The darker surface is harmless and many owners keep it.

## 2. Polishing

To restore the shine, rub with half a lemon dipped in salt, or with a copper polish, then rinse and dry.

## 3. What to avoid

Never use a dishwasher or a scouring pad on copper.

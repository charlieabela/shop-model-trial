# Supplier specification for the 24 cm copper sauté pan (CP24)

Issued by Harbour Cookware, a fictional supplier.

## 1. Materials and dimensions

The CP24 has a copper exterior, a stainless steel cooking surface and a 24 cm rim. It belongs to the shop's Premium range.

## 2. Hob compatibility

The pan works on gas and conventional electric hobs. Its base does not work on induction without a separate induction adapter.

# Buying guide for sauté pans

## 1. Choosing a pan for an induction hob

Check the exact model's specification for a magnetic base or an explicit induction rating. Pan diameter and the material of the cooking surface alone do not establish compatibility.

## 2. Choosing a pan for easy cleaning

Check the exact model's care sheet for dishwasher guidance. Do not assume that two pans with similar names have the same care instructions.

# Care of woven and plant-fibre products

## 1. Cleaning

Wipe with a damp cloth and leave to dry in the air. These products are not washed under running water and are not put in a dishwasher.

## 2. No oiling

Plant fibre is not treated as wood is. Do not oil it, because oil darkens the fibre unevenly and leaves it sticky.

## 3. Storage

Keep the item dry and out of direct sun, which makes the fibre brittle.

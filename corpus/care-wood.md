# Care of wooden products

## 1. Washing

Wash by hand in warm water with a little washing-up liquid and dry at once with a cloth. Never leave the item to soak and never put it in a dishwasher, because the heat and the water split the grain.

## 2. Oiling

Rub in a food-safe mineral oil once a month, or whenever the surface looks pale and dry. Leave the oil overnight and wipe off what has not been absorbed.

## 3. Stains and smells

Rub the surface with half a lemon and coarse salt, rinse and dry. Do not use bleach.

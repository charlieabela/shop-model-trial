# Care of cast iron

## 1. Seasoning

A bare cast-iron surface is seasoned before first use: coat it thinly with vegetable oil and heat it for an hour at 200 degrees. An enamelled piece is not seasoned.

## 2. Washing

Wash by hand in hot water without soaking, and dry at once on a low heat. Do not use a dishwasher.

## 3. Rust

Scrub the rust away with wire wool, wash, dry and season again.

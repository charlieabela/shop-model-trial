# Warranty and faults

## 1. Legal guarantee

Every item carries the 2-year legal guarantee for faults present at delivery.

## 2. Premium warranty

Premium range items also carry a 5-year manufacturer warranty against manufacturing defects.

## 3. Not faults

Wear from normal use, damage from misuse, and dishwasher damage to items marked hand wash only are not faults.

## 4. What you get

For a fault we repair, replace or refund the item. Within the first 6 months we assume the fault was present at delivery.

# Care of terracotta

## 1. Before first use

Soak in water for an hour before first use, so that the clay does not draw water from the soil.

## 2. Frost

Terracotta is porous and cracks in frost when wet. Bring it indoors or raise it off the ground in winter.

## 3. White marks

A white bloom on the outside is salt from the water and is brushed off when dry.

# Standard returns

## 1. Who can return

Any customer who bought an item from the shop's own stock can return it. Items sold by marketplace sellers follow the seller's own return terms, set out in the seller rules.

## 2. The return window

You can return an item within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method. The window counts calendar days, and the delivery date is the date on the courier's proof of delivery.

## 3. Condition

Returned items must be unused, complete and in their original packaging. Items returned used or incomplete may be refused, or refunded in part after inspection.

## 4. Return shipping

Return shipping is free within Malta and Gozo when you use the prepaid label from your account. If you arrange your own courier, you pay for it and we do not refund that cost.

## 5. Exceptions

Some items have different rules. Premium range items, personalised items, sale items and opened food products each have their own policy, which takes precedence over this one.

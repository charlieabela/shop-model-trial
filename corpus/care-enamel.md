# Care of enamelled steel

## 1. Use

Safe in an oven up to 250 degrees and under a grill. Not for use in a microwave.

## 2. Washing

Dishwasher safe. Soak burnt food in warm water and do not use metal scourers.

## 3. Chips

A chip in the enamel on the rim does not affect use. A chip on the cooking surface means the item should be replaced.

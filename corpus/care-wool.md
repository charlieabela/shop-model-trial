# Care of woollen products

## 1. Washing

Wash by hand in cool water, or on a wool cycle at no more than 30 degrees, with a detergent made for wool.

## 2. Drying

Dry flat on a towel, away from radiators. Never tumble dry, because wool shrinks and felts.

## 3. Finer wools

Where a separate care note exists for a finer wool, that note takes precedence over this one.

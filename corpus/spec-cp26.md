# Supplier specification for the 26 cm stainless sauté pan (CP26)

Issued by Harbour Cookware, a fictional supplier.

## 1. Materials and dimensions

The CP26 has a stainless steel body and a 26 cm rim. It belongs to the shop's standard range.

## 2. Hob compatibility

The pan has a magnetic base suitable for induction. It also works on gas and conventional electric hobs.
